Key tips for organising your furniture transport in Mallorca

Transport in Mallorca Professional logistics Removals
Mallorca Transportes · August 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Planning to move furniture in Mallorca? In this article we give you practical tips for organising efficient, safe transport with no surprises. Ideal for homes, businesses or renovations.

1. Measure before you move

Before booking the service, measure large pieces of furniture and compare them with the access points: doors, hallways, lifts and stairs. This way we can let you know in advance if any piece needs to be dismantled.

2. Protect what's fragile

Glass, mirrors, appliances and fine wood pieces need specific protection. We use packing blankets, film and corner protectors so everything arrives at its destination in perfect condition.

3. Empty drawers and cabinets

Furniture weighs less and is easier to handle safely when it's empty. It also stops the contents from shifting or getting damaged during the journey.

4. Plan the timing

In areas with heavy traffic or narrow streets, like the centre of Palma or the old towns of some villages, choosing the right time slot makes loading and unloading much easier.

5. Rely on professionals for large pieces

Sofas, wardrobes, kitchens or bulky appliances require technique and, at times, dismantling. An experienced team reduces the risk of knocks to walls, door frames and the furniture itself.

6. Ask for a fixed quote

That way you avoid last-minute surprises. At Mallorca Transportes we assess the volume, distance and access to give you a clear price from the very first moment.
