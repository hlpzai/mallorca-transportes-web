Furniture transport for businesses in Mallorca: punctuality, safety and professional service

Business & offices Furniture transport Professional logistics
Mallorca Transportes · July 18, 2026 · 4 min read

Need to transport furniture or equipment for your business in Mallorca? Discover our professional service, fast and tailored to businesses.

Why punctuality matters in business transport

When transport is part of a business's operations — a shop receiving new furniture, a restaurant renovating its dining room, an office expanding its workstations — every hour of delay has a cost. That's why we work to fixed schedules and confirm the delivery window in advance.

Safety in every delivery

Business furniture is often a significant investment: office desks, counters, custom shelving, light machinery or hospitality equipment. Every shipment is packed and secured according to the type of material, and all our services include transport insurance.

A professional service, from start to finish

Our team coordinates directly with you or with the person in charge at the destination, confirms access and timing, and adapts to the building or shopping centre's rules where needed (loading docks, restricted hours, access permits).

Common cases we handle

  • Delivery and setup of new furniture in offices and commercial premises
  • Moving furniture between different sites of the same company
  • Transporting materials from a factory or supplier to a warehouse
  • One-off equipment deliveries for events or trade fairs

If your business needs a trusted transport provider in Mallorca, get in touch and we'll put together a proposal tailored to your activity.
