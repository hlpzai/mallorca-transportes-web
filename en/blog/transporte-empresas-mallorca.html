Professional transport for businesses in Mallorca: tailored solutions for your company

Business transport Mallorca Professional furniture logistics Commercial transport
Mallorca Transportes · August 28, 2026 · 4 min read

Do you run a business in Mallorca and need to move furniture, equipment or work materials? At Mallorca Transportes we offer a specialised service for businesses looking for fast, careful logistics solutions tailored to their needs, without interrupting daily operations.

Transport for businesses in any sector

In recent years we've helped dozens of businesses across Mallorca carry out office moves and internal relocations without slowing down their revenue. Some of the sectors we regularly work with:

  • Offices and professional practices
  • Clinics and medical centres
  • Shops and retail businesses
  • Restaurants and cafés
  • Renovation and interior design companies
  • Real estate agencies and developers

We know that time is money for your business, which is why we prioritise punctuality and efficiency in every job.

What does our professional transport service include?

  • Packing and protection of furniture and sensitive materials
  • Safe loading and unloading, with specialised staff
  • Transport in adapted vehicles
  • Extra staff reinforcement when volume requires it
  • Option to work outside business hours
  • Insurance included with every service

We also offer recurring or one-off services, depending on what your business needs.

Renovating or moving premises?

If you're renovating your office or relocating your shop to another part of the island, our team takes care of the entire logistics process so you can focus on your business. We can even help you dismantle and reassemble the furniture if you need us to.

Coverage across all of Mallorca

We serve businesses in Palma, Marratxí, Inca, Manacor, Calvià, Llucmajor and the rest of the island's municipalities. We'll travel to wherever you are, adapting to the logistics of your building, premises or warehouse.
