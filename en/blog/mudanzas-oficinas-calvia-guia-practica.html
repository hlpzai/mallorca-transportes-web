Office removals in Calvià: planning and keys to success

Relocating an office in Calvià involves much more than moving furniture and boxes. Planning, coordinating with service providers and adapting to local schedules are essential to avoid disrupting business activity. At Mallorca Transportes, we help businesses in Calvià and the surrounding area manage office moves with professionalism and personalised attention.

Why choose a professional service for your office move?

  • Local experience: We know Calvià's urban layout inside out, from business parks to offices in residential areas, which speeds up access and any necessary permits.
  • Safe transport: We handle IT equipment, furniture and files with appropriate packing and protection systems specific to each type of material.
  • Dismantling and assembly: We offer a full service to dismantle and reassemble desks, shelving and cabinets, so the new office can be up and running as soon as possible.

Basic steps for an office move in Calvià

  • 1. Inventory and planning: Before the move, make an inventory of all equipment and furniture. Decide what gets moved, what gets donated or recycled, and plan the layout in the new location.
  • 2. Coordinating with providers: Give advance notice to internet, cleaning and security providers so the transition is smooth with no service interruptions.
  • 3. Packing and labelling: Use sturdy boxes and label each one by department or area of the new office. This makes unloading and organising at the destination much easier.
  • 4. Dismantling and protecting equipment: IT and electronic equipment needs special packing to prevent damage. Our team handles this process with the right materials.
  • 5. Transport and assembly: The move is carried out at an agreed time, with the option of doing it outside peak hours or on a weekend if needed to minimise the impact on business activity.

What additional services might you need for your office move?

  • Express removals: For urgent or last-minute moves, you can rely on a fast, secure service.
  • Transport between the Balearic Islands: If your business has premises on more than one island, we handle transport between Mallorca, Menorca, Ibiza and Formentera.
  • Waste management and recycling: We responsibly dispose of old furniture and waste, working with authorised waste managers.

Common mistakes when relocating an office in Calvià

  • Not booking the service with enough notice, especially in high season or around the tax year-end.
  • Not informing employees and suppliers of the moving timeline.
  • Leaving the disconnection and protection of IT equipment until the last minute.
